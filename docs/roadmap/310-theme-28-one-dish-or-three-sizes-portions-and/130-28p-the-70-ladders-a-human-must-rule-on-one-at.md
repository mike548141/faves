- [ ] **28p — The 70 ladders a human must rule on, one at a time** `[L][data]

  **Measured at `e50c0ee`.** Of the 133 split-row groups, **70 have siblings
  that differ in more than name and price**: `tags` diverge in **58**, `desc`
  in **46**, `prices` in **6** (a group is often both). Every one is a
  judgement about whether the venue is selling one dish or two.

  🔎 **`28a` is right about these and wrong about the other 63, and this item
  is where its reasoning survives.** Its argument — *"a size variant needs its
  own desc, tags, section, availability and `addOns`, and once it needs all
  five it **is** a dish"* — is a test, and applying it to the corpus splits it
  53/47. Do not discard the argument; use it as the rubric.

  🛑 **36 of these 70 are the dietary-claim groups from `28i` and cannot be
  touched until that is ruled.** They cost 58 rows a claim they carry today.

  **The rest divide by why they diverge, and each wants a different answer:**
  - **`desc` differs but says the same thing** — Abrakebabra's kebabs read
    *"Served in white flat bread…"* plus the meat. The description is the
    choice restated; it merges into the option's own words.
  - **`desc` differs materially** — Regal's Half/Whole duck, 1841's Ironside
    200g/400g. The base keeps what is true of both; the gram weight belongs to
    the option.
  - **`prices` differ in shape** (6 groups) — a per-channel price on one
    sibling and not the other (ADR 0089). A `selects` option carrying a price
    per door is a shape neither ADR describes. 🎯 **Likely a further ask.**
  - **Not a ladder at all.** `28b` already found these and they must be
    honoured: Heineken on-tap / bottle / **0.0 alcohol-free** is not a size
    ladder, and Sushi Bi's Platters 1–9 are nine compositions. `28h`'s
    enumerator will offer some of these; **refusing one is a result, not a
    gap.**

  ✅ **What proves it landed.** Not a count — a **decision recorded per
  group**. Each group ends in one of three states, written down: merged (with
  the same evidence `28o` demands), refused with a reason, or deferred to the
  owner. `28h`'s enumerator reports zero groups in no state at all, which is
  the only claim worth making here.
